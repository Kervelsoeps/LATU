LATU Online recognizes the importance of protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with Google Firebase (Cloud storage and Auth) and other essential service providers as necessary to run the Service.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can delete your account data through the game settings or by contacting us.
